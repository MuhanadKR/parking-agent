# Harborline Parking: Permits

Permits let regular visitors, residents and commuters park without paying hourly rates. A permit is tied to a license plate, not to a specific space, except for reserved permits.

## Permit types and prices

| Permit | Price | Where and when it is valid |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Standard, Lot A | $180 per month | Lot A, any unreserved space, 24 hours a day |
| Monthly Standard, Lot B | $95 per month | Lot B, any space, during opening hours |
| Reserved, Lot A | $260 per month | Lot A, spaces A-03 to A-06 only, 24 hours a day |
| Night Resident, Lot A | $45 per month | Lot A, from 19:00 to 08:00 every day |
| Visitor Day Pass | $10 per day | Lot A, for one calendar day |

## Night Resident permit

The Night Resident permit is for people who live nearby and need to park overnight. It is valid only from 19:00 to 08:00. Outside those hours, standard rates apply. Only 6 Night Resident permits are issued at a time, and a waitlist is kept when they are all taken. You must provide proof of address.

## Visitor Day Pass

Night Resident permit holders can buy up to 5 Visitor Day Passes per month for their guests. Each pass covers one vehicle at Lot A for one calendar day.

## How to apply

Apply online or by email. You need your vehicle registration and a photo ID. Applications are processed within 3 business days. If a permit type is full, you are added to a waitlist.

## Permit rules

- A permit can list up to 2 license plates, but only one registered vehicle can be parked on the permit at a time.
- Changing a registered plate costs $5.
- Permits are billed on the 1st of each month and renew automatically.
- To cancel, give 30 days' written notice.
- A permit is valid only at the lot it was issued for. A Lot B permit does not work at Lot A.
- Permits cannot be transferred to another person.
- Monthly Standard and Reserved permit holders are exempt from the 24-hour maximum stay at Lot A.
- A permit does not cover electric vehicle charging fees or any fine.
