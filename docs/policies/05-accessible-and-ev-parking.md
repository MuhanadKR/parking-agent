# Harborline Parking: Accessible and Electric Vehicle Parking

This document covers the accessible spaces and the electric vehicle (EV) charging spaces at our lots.

## Accessible parking

### Where the spaces are

- **Riverside Lot A:** spaces A-01 and A-02, the closest spaces to the exit. A-01 is van-accessible and has an 8-foot side access aisle.
- **Market Lot B:** space B-01.

### Who can use them

Accessible spaces are for vehicles displaying a valid accessible parking placard, or vehicles with a registered accessible license plate. The placard must hang from the rear-view mirror and be visible. Accessible spaces cannot be reserved in advance. Using one without either a valid placard or a registered accessible license plate carries a $250 fine.

### Placard discount

Drivers with a valid placard pay 50% of the hourly rate at both lots. The discount applies to hourly rates only. It does not apply to the daily maximum, the Lot A overnight flat rate, the Lot B weekend flat rate or the early-bird rate.

## Electric vehicle charging

### Where the chargers are

Chargers are at Riverside Lot A only, in spaces A-19 and A-20. Market Lot B has no charging. The chargers are Level 2 chargers with a power of 7 kW and are available 24 hours a day, the same as the lot.

### Starting a session

Start charging with the Harborline app or with an RFID charging card. Normal parking charges, or a valid permit, still apply while you are in the space.

### Charging fees

- **Energy:** $0.35 per kWh, on top of parking charges.
- **Maximum session:** 4 hours.
- **Idle fee:** if your vehicle stays plugged in after charging finishes, an idle fee of $0.50 per minute starts 15 minutes after the session ends. The fee is capped at $20 per session.

### Rules

Only electric vehicles that are charging may use A-19 and A-20. A non-electric vehicle in these spaces is fined $75.

### Faulty chargers

Report a faulty charger in the app or to customer support. You are not billed for energy that was not delivered because of a charger fault.
