# Harborline Parking: Lost Tickets, Refunds and Disputes

This document explains what to do if you lose a ticket, how refunds work, and how to appeal a fine.

## Lost tickets

Only Market Lot B uses tickets. Riverside Lot A has no tickets, because it uses license plate recognition.

If you lose your Lot B ticket, you have two options:

1. **Pay the lost ticket fee.** The fee is a flat $20.
2. **Prove your entry time.** Give staff your license plate and an approximate entry time within a 2-hour window. Staff check the entry camera footage and charge you the normal rate for your actual stay. Footage is kept for 7 days. After 7 days, only the flat $20 fee is available.

## Paid for the wrong plate

At Lot A, payment is linked to the license plate you enter. If you entered the wrong plate, contact us in the app or by phone within 24 hours and we correct it at no charge. After 24 hours, the payment cannot be moved or refunded.

## Refunds

- **Duplicate charges:** refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- **Overcharges at a kiosk or gate:** once verified, refunded within 5 to 7 business days.
- **Unused prepaid time:** not refundable if you leave early.
- **Permits:** refunds are given for unused full months only, and only with 30 days' written notice. We do not refund part of a month.

## Gate or kiosk faults

If you cannot leave because of a gate or kiosk fault, call the 24-hour line. Staff open the gate remotely and waive the fee for that visit. If you believe your vehicle was damaged, send a written claim with photos within 14 days.

## Appealing a fine

1. **Deadline:** appeal within 14 days of the notice date.
2. **How:** use the online form, or email customer support with your notice number (HP-######) and your evidence, such as photos or payment receipts.
3. **Decision:** we reply within 10 business days.
4. **If the appeal is upheld:** the fine is cancelled.
5. **If the appeal is denied:** the full amount is due within 14 days. The reduced amount is no longer available.
6. **Escalation:** within 7 days of a denial, you can escalate once to the Parking Review Panel. The panel's decision is final.
