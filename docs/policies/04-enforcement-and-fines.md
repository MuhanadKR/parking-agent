# Harborline Parking: Enforcement and Fines

This document explains when parking rules are enforced, what the fines are, and what happens if a fine is not paid.

## Enforcement hours

- **Riverside Lot A:** payment is enforced from 07:00 to 23:00 every day. From 23:00 to 07:00, vehicles are checked for the overnight rate or a valid permit.
- **Market Lot B:** payment is enforced during opening hours, from 06:00 to 22:00 every day.

Rules are enforced with license plate recognition cameras and by patrol staff.

## Fine schedule

| Violation | Amount |
|---|---|
| No payment and no valid permit | $45, reduced to $30 if paid within 7 days of the notice |
| Staying more than 30 minutes past the paid time | $25 |
| Parking in a permit-only space (A-03 to A-06) without a Reserved permit | $60 |
| Using an accessible space without a valid placard | $250 |
| Parking a non-electric vehicle in an EV space (A-19 and A-20) | $75 |
| Exceeding the 24-hour maximum stay at Lot A | $50 for each additional 24-hour period |
| Blocking a driving lane or occupying two spaces | $40 |
| Remaining in Lot B after 22:30 | $30 after-hours fee |

## Overstay allowance

No overstay fine is issued until 30 minutes after your paid time ends. This allowance is separate from the 15-minute free grace period at entry.

## Fine notices

Each notice has a number in the format HP-######. Keep it, because you need it to pay or appeal.

## Paying a fine

A fine is due within 30 days of the notice date. If it is not paid by then, a late fee of $20 is added. You can pay online, in the Harborline app, or at the Lot A kiosk.

## Towing

A vehicle that stays at Lot A for more than 72 hours in total, or that blocks access to the lot, may be towed. The tow fee is $120, plus storage of $15 per day charged by the tow yard. Paying the tow fee does not cancel the parking fines already issued.

## Appeals

You can appeal any fine. See the refunds and disputes document for the appeal process and deadlines.
