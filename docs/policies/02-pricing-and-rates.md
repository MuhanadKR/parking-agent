# Harborline Parking: Pricing and Rates

All prices are in US dollars and include tax. Rates are posted at each lot entrance and are reviewed once a year.

## Grace period

The first 15 minutes after entering either lot are free. The grace period applies once per entry and is meant for drop-offs and quick stops. Parking longer than 15 minutes is billed from the moment you entered.

## How time is billed

After the grace period, time is billed in 15-minute increments, and any part of an increment is rounded up to the next one.

## Riverside Lot A rates

Lot A charges differ for daytime and overnight stays.

- **Daytime (07:00 to 23:00):** $4.00 per hour for the first 2 hours, then $3.00 per hour for each additional hour.
- **Daily maximum:** $24.00 per calendar day for daytime parking.
- **Overnight flat rate (23:00 to 07:00):** $8.00 for a stay that covers the overnight period.

## Market Lot B rates

- **Weekday hourly rate:** $2.50 per hour.
- **Daily maximum:** $15.00 per day of operation.
- **Weekend flat rate:** $10.00 per day on Saturday and Sunday, instead of the hourly rate.
- **Early-bird rate:** $9.00 flat on weekdays (Monday to Friday) for vehicles that enter before 09:00 and leave between 15:30 and 19:00.

## Permit holders

Permit holders do not pay hourly rates at the lot named on their permit. Electric vehicle charging fees still apply. See the permits document for permit prices.

## Discounts

Drivers with a valid accessible parking placard pay a reduced hourly rate. See the accessible and electric vehicle parking document for details.

## Paying

You can pay at the Lot A kiosk, at the Lot B exit gate, or in the Harborline app. Cash is accepted at the Lot A kiosk only. Lot B accepts cards and the app. If you pay in the app, enter your license plate carefully, because payment is linked to the plate you enter.
